TechFest 2026: Hackout

About the Event

Hackout is CampusPulse's flagship 24-hour hackathon, bringing together the sharpest coders, designers, and problem-solvers on campus. Teams of up to 4 will build a working prototype from scratch, tackling real-world problem statements across web, AI, and sustainability tracks.

Mentors from partner startups will be available throughout the night, and the event closes with live demos judged by a panel of industry professionals and faculty.

Schedule

10:00 AM Check-in & team formation
11:00 AM Problem statements released
12:00 PM Hacking begins
Next day, 10:00 AM Submissions close
Next day, 1:00 PM Live demos & judging
Next day, 4:00 PM Prize distribution

Rules

  • Teams must have 2–4 members, all currently enrolled students.
  • All code must be written during the 24-hour window — pre-built projects are not eligible.
  • Use of open-source libraries and APIs is allowed and encouraged.
  • Each team must submit a working demo and a short presentation before the deadline.
