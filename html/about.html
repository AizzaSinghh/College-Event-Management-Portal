About Us

Built By Students,
For Students.

CampusPulse started as a simple idea — stop letting great events get lost in group chats and forgotten notice boards.

Our Story

Why We Built This.

Every semester, dozens of great events happen on campus — hackathons, fests, workshops, matches — and most students only hear about them after they've already happened.

CampusPulse brings every event, every club, and every deadline into one place, so no one has to rely on a dozen different WhatsApp groups just to know what's going on.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Simplicity

One portal instead of ten different sources — finding an event should take seconds, not scrolling.

Community

Clubs and students should be able to find each other easily, without needing an insider connection.

Transparency

Clear event details, honest deadlines, no last-minute surprises about venues or timings.
