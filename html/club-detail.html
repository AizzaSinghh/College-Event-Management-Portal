Robotics Club

About the Club

The Robotics Club has been building, breaking, and rebuilding since 2019. From line-following bots for first-years to full autonomous rovers for national competitions, we cover every level of robotics — no prior experience required to join.

We compete at inter-college tech fests throughout the year and run a peer-mentorship system where senior members teach juniors the electronics, coding, and mechanical design skills needed to build a working robot from scratch.

What You'll Do

  • Weekly hands-on build sessions in the robotics lab
  • Workshops on Arduino, sensors, and basic mechanical design
  • Team-based projects leading up to national competitions
  • Access to the club's tools, components, and 3D printer

Meeting Schedule

Tuesdays 5:00 PM — General build session, Robotics Lab
Fridays 4:00 PM — Beginner workshop (open to all years)
Monthly Last Saturday — Progress showcase & demo day

Achievements

  • 2nd place, National Robotics Championship, 2025
  • Best Innovation Award, TechFest Circuit, 2024
  • Finalist, Inter-College Robotics League, 2023 & 2024
